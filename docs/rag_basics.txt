Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) retrieves relevant text passages from a document store before asking an LLM to answer, instead of relying purely on the model's parametric memory. A basic RAG pipeline embeds documents into vectors ahead of time, embeds the incoming query the same way, ranks documents by cosine similarity, and inserts the top matches into the prompt as context. Chunking long documents into smaller passages (a few hundred words each) usually improves retrieval quality over embedding whole files.
