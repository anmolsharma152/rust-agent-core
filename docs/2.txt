### **PHASE 1: FlowCV Design & Customization (The Engine)**

Before you paste a single word, configure the document settings.

1. **Template:** Select **Precision Line**. This gives you the clean horizontal dividers under each section heading.
2. **Layout:** Choose **Top to Bottom (Single Column)**. Do not use sidebars; they mess with ATS parsing and waste horizontal space needed for your technical bullets.
3. **Typography:** Set the font to **Inter** or **Roboto**. Set the font size to **10pt or 11pt**.
4. **Colors:** Set the primary text to pure black (`#000000`). If FlowCV allows a primary color for links/headings, use your electric blue (`#3b82f6`) sparingly, or just stick to black for maximum corporate compliance.
5. **Margins:** Set margins to **Standard** or **Compact** (if you need more room to fit everything on exactly one page).
6. **Icons:** In the Profile/Contact section settings, turn Icons **ON** and select the minimalist/outline style.
